Engine Calculation Rules
Gross Earnings Formula
Gross = Basic + HRA + Special Allowances + Overtime Pay
Overtime Pay = (Basic Salary / (Working Days × 8)) × OT Hours × 1.5
Deductions Formula
Deductions = LOP + Provident Fund + Professional Tax + Income Tax (TDS)
  • LOP: (Gross Salary / Total Working Days) × Unpaid Leaves
  • PF: 12.00% of Monthly Basic Salary
  • PT: Fixed Statutory slab (₹200.00 / mo)
  • TDS: Progressive tax slabs based on annualized gross
Net Disbursed Take-Home
Net Salary = Gross Salary - Total Deductions
